Design 3 mobile banking home screens, side by side, same content, different layouts, no labels. Canvas: light grey #EDF0F5, generous space between phones, extra empty space at the top for labels. Style: light mode only, brand #2251FF blue, text black #0E1B3D and grey #7A8399, no blue text, fonts Manrope for UI and Bricolage Grotesque for balances, generous gaps between sections. Label the three phones above the frames — left 'ChatGPT', centre 'Claude', right 'Figma Make' — in bold type. Content: header 'Good morning, Lulamile M, LM avatar, bell with a red dot, settings icon'; ZAR account, R1,850.00, In R3,400.00 / Out R59.48; actions 'Add money, Send, Convert, More'; transactions 'Apple.com/bill -R37.49 Declined', 'Salary deposit +R3,400.00', 'Netflix subscription -R15.49', 'Nando's -R6.50'; nav 'Home, Cards, Invest, Accounts'. Screen 1: full blue banner card with wallet pill, big balance with faded decimals, white in/out progress bar; pill chip actions, Add money in dark navy; vertical timeline transactions with coloured status dots; standard bottom bar with labels, Home in blue. Screen 2: blue banner card with two lighter cards stacked behind it, balance and in/out bar; 4 small rounded-square icon buttons with labels below, Add money in blue; clean list transactions with dividers; floating white pill nav with soft shadow, Home as a blue pill. Screen 3: off-white filled banner card, dark balance, blue strip at the bottom with In and Out; one segmented action bar, Add money in blue.
