04 / Before you ask

Common questions.

What does a project cost?

Starting prices are on our Pricing page — for example, websites start from R18,000. After a short conversation you get a clear written quote covering scope, deliverables and timing before any work begins. Nothing starts until you approve it.

How do we start working together?

Send an enquiry, message us on WhatsApp or book a meeting. We talk through your goals, then send a written quote covering scope, deliverables, responsibilities, budget and timing. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Who will work on my project?

Our core team of three: our founder (strategy, UX/UI design and frontend), a graphic designer and animator, and a certified backend and AI engineer. You work with us directly from the first conversation to launch — meet the team on our About page.

What does working with you look like?

Review points are planned into your scope, so you see progress and give feedback before the next stage. Questions can go through WhatsApp, email or a booked call, and the agreed journeys are tested end to end before launch.

How long does a project take?

Smaller design or setup work can move quickly; connected systems take longer. Timing is discussed and agreed before work starts, and kept visible through planned review points.

Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?

Yes. LulaZel Digital Studio works remotely with businesses worldwide, whatever your time zone.

Do I need a technical brief before we talk?

No. Bring the problem you want to solve — goals, examples and what you use today all help, but a complete technical brief is not required.

What do hosting, payments and AI cost?

Those services come from the providers you choose and are billed under their terms. We set them up, connect them and manage them to the agreed scope; their fees are yours to pay directly to them.

What happens after launch?

You receive everything we agreed — access, assets and documentation. Ongoing support and maintenance options are available and agreed before the work starts.
