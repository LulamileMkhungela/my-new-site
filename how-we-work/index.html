01 / How a project runs

Six steps from first message to launch.

  1. 01

    Talk it through

    Tell us what you’re building and what isn’t working. No brief required — goals, examples and what you use today are enough to start.

  2. 02

    Agree a written scope

    You get a written quote covering deliverables, responsibilities, budget and timing. Nothing starts until you approve it.

  3. 03

    Design the experience

    Journeys, content structure and interfaces are shaped around your customers and the way your business works, then reviewed with you.

  4. 04

    Build and connect

    Design and development move together. Suitable providers are selected and configured; forms, bookings, payments and other integrations are connected to the agreed scope.

  5. 05

    Test and launch

    The agreed journeys are tested end to end before a considered launch.

  6. 06

    Hand over and support

    You receive the access, assets and documentation agreed. Ongoing support and maintenance options are agreed upfront.

  • Review points are planned into your scope, so you see progress and give feedback before the next stage.
  • Questions can go through WhatsApp, email or a booked call — whichever suits you.
  • Johannesburg-based, working with businesses across South Africa and remotely, whatever your time zone.

02 / Who builds what

Everyone knows their part.

Our founder

Strategy, journeys and interface design, frontend development, provider setup, testing and launch — from the first conversation to handover.

Graphic designer & animator

Brand visuals, social and print graphics, motion graphics and animated explainers.

Backend & AI engineer

Databases, APIs, cloud infrastructure and AI features, built and reviewed by our certified AI and backend engineer.

You

Your goals, content such as copy, photos and product details, brand assets, feedback at each review point and final approvals.

Your chosen providers

Hosting, domains, payment gateways, booking, email and AI services. Their fees are billed by them under their own terms.

04 / Questions

Before you get in touch.

How do we start working together?

Send an enquiry, message us on WhatsApp or book a meeting. We talk through your goals, then send a written quote covering scope, deliverables, responsibilities, budget and timing. Nothing starts until you approve it.

Who will work on my project?

Our core team of three: our founder (strategy, UX/UI design and frontend), a graphic designer and animator, and a certified backend and AI engineer. You work with us directly from the first conversation to launch — meet the team on our About page.

What does working with you look like?

Review points are planned into your scope, so you see progress and give feedback before the next stage. Questions can go through WhatsApp, email or a booked call, and the agreed journeys are tested end to end before launch.

Do I need a technical brief before we talk?

No. Bring the problem you want to solve — goals, examples and what you use today all help, but a complete technical brief is not required.

What happens after launch?

You receive everything we agreed — access, assets and documentation. Ongoing support and maintenance options are available and agreed before the work starts.
