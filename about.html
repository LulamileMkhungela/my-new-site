Your business deserves more than a good-looking screen. LulaZel brings design and development together to turn your ideas into websites, apps and connected systems that look right, work properly and support the way you do business.

One partner. From idea to launch.

Good design opens the door. Great development makes it work.

LulaZel is an independent digital partner combining product design and development from the first conversation to the finished experience. The focus is simple: turn a business challenge into something useful, considered and ready to work in the real world.

Every project starts with your goals, not a template. Understanding your customers and the way your business operates shapes the journeys, interfaces and technology. Design and development move together, so the thinking behind the experience carries through into the finished product.

Behind the screen, the pieces need to connect. LulaZel brings together websites, online stores, custom business systems, automation and practical AI around the work you need to get done. Hosting is selected and configured with suitable providers; forms, bookings, payments and other integrations are scoped around your requirements.

One connected approach means fewer gaps between the idea, the design and the build. Clear scope, regular reviews, testing and a considered launch keep you involved without making you manage every technical detail. The result is a digital experience built to serve your customers and make your business easier to run.

Based in South Africa. Working with businesses worldwide. Your goals, your time zone, one connected approach.

How we work

Principles you can rely on.

  1. 01

    Your brief leads

    Every project starts with your goals and constraints, not a template. The plan is shaped around what you actually need.

  2. 02

    Clear communication

    Agreed scope, regular reviews and plain language from the first conversation to launch. You always know what happens next.

  3. 03

    Involved at every stage

    Design and development move together, and you review the work at each stage or iteration. No black box and no big reveal — your feedback shapes every pass.

  4. 04

    Iterate in the open

    Review points are planned into the work. Feedback arrives early, when changes are small and affordable.

  5. 05

    Tested before it ships

    Checked across devices and screen sizes before launch, with your feedback shaping the final pass.

  6. 06

    Supported after launch

    A considered handover, clear access and documentation, with optional ongoing support agreed before the work starts.

Honest scope

What LulaZel doesn’t take on.

If a request is not the right fit, that is said up front — saving you time, money and frustration.

  • 01

    Managed IT infrastructure

    Networks, servers and hardware are not offered. Hosting is set up through the provider you choose, to an agreed scope.

  • 02

    Print, photography & events

    No printing, photo shoots, videography or event coverage. Brand assets can be supplied and applied to the digital work.

  • 03

    Full rebrands

    Basic graphic design supports your existing identity. Complete rebranding exercises are outside the current offer.

  • 04

    Provider costs, promised free

    Hosting, payment and AI services are billed by those providers under their terms. Those costs are always agreed upfront, never guessed.

AI governance

Your business data is never exposed to AI

Practical AI is part of the offer, but your business data is not handed to an AI service to be trained on or guessed at. Every AI feature runs on the providers you choose, with a clear governance practice: what data goes in, what comes back, who can see it and how it is stored is agreed and documented upfront. Where AI is used, it stays inside the agreed scope, with human review before anything reaches your customers. No business data is used to train models, and no AI service is given access to your systems beyond what the agreed task needs.

  • 01

    AI runs only through providers you approve, never with blanket access to your systems

  • 02

    What data is used, how it is stored and who can see it is agreed and documented before work starts

  • 03

    Human review sits between the AI and anything your customers or team see

  • 04

    No business data is used to train models — AI is a tool, not an audience for your data
