Ambient fallback loops
======================
focus-1.mp3  Lo-fi focus loop
focus-2.mp3  Quiet piano loop
focus-3.mp3  Ambient drift loop

These are short, procedurally synthesized fallback loops generated for this
site with NumPy and the @breezystack/lamejs MP3 encoder. They contain no
third-party samples or recordings. The ambient player tries the reference
site's original Pixabay track URLs first and falls back to these local loops
when a remote track cannot load, so the control still works offline or behind
restrictive networks.

Generation: 24 seconds, 22.05 kHz mono, 64 kbps MP3, deterministic seed.
